ABOUT THIS PROTOTYPE

Experience first. Decide next.

Choose a state to see its light behaviour. Apply a fixed direction and elevation to compare views. At 0°, the camera is level with the ring. Web emitters are flat surfaces, so this is an edge-on diagnostic view rather than a physical visibility prediction. Drag to orbit, or use the buttons for a top view and to hide the cover. The mouse wheel scrolls the page; pinch gestures zoom the model.

Capture hold simulates ongoing capture until explicitly stopped. Its red indication persists through state changes, dimming and pause. Selecting Sleep does not stop an active hold; Defaults resets the whole simulation. The Capture button can play a photo cue during a hold without releasing the red indication. Stopping a hold during a photo cue retains red until that cue finishes. Capture uses illustrative timing: red appears first, followed by white capture and acknowledgement cues, then the currently selected state resumes. Changing states during capture updates the return target without overriding red. Repeated capture triggers are ignored. Capture exits Conversation autoplay; it resumes the selected state, not the remaining sequence.

Conversation automatically plays Ready → Listening → Processing → Speaking → Ready. Choose any state to leave the sequence. Error repeats until you select another state; choose Ready to simulate a resolved error. Wake is a 1.5-second entry animation into Ready, available after selecting Sleep. It is not a separate state. Speaking and Error each offer the Week 3 baseline and two spatial/timing candidates. Switch variants at the same phase or use Replay from start for matched playback. Speaking never accesses the microphone. Default Conversation lasts 18 seconds and includes 5.6 seconds of stable Listening, excluding transitions. Breathing-period or transition changes exit autoplay.

The cover and base come from the accepted Blender source. Adjust LED count to compare layouts across all animations. Light zones visualise interaction behaviour, not validated PCB geometry, optical mixing, or physical brightness. Privacy arcs are specified in degrees and mapped outward to whole logical sectors. The mapped total includes gaps and does not prove visibility. Front is web zone 01; hardware indexing remains unconfirmed.

Export JSON to save the parameters and provisional LED count for future driver integration. The export does not claim hardware validation.
