Speaking B retains opposed speech patches, reducing dependence on one front-facing region. Error B keeps a broken-ring signature between pulses so an unresolved fault remains distinct from uniform Ready. These are design recommendations, pending review without labels and physical validation.

Listening remains the only uniform breathing state. Processing retains its moving 90° arc. Wake is now a 1.5-second entry animation into Ready. The default conversation lasts 18 seconds, including two complete Listening breaths (5.6 seconds) after its entry transition.
