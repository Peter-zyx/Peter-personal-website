Built with Cici from August to September 2026, CP-APP brings shared tasks, expenses, wishes, personal notes and weekly reflection into a private space for two people.

The native SwiftUI client and the Next.js Web app reuse Supabase accounts, PostgreSQL data and access rules. Pairing does not grant location access; each person chooses whether and for how long to share. Optional AI journals use selected records and a user-supplied provider, with an explicit preview before generation.

As of 16 September 2026, the Web app is deployed and development builds are installed on both collaborators’ iPhones. App Store distribution, native background remote notifications and extended two-device field validation remain future work.
