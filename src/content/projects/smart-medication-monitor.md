Completed in August 2026, Smart Medication Monitor brings load-cell sensing, camera action recognition, embedded inference and an iOS interface into one working engineering prototype.

The case study uses the final 32-page research report and the project repository. Weight removal and visible actions provide supporting event evidence; neither confirms swallowing.
