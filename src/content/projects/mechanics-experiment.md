## Overview

This project investigated how a ball sliding down an inclined tube rebounds after impact, with the aim of estimating the inclination angle that maximises rebound height.

## Outcome

The final report combined experimental trials, averaged measurements, outlier removal, force analysis, and mathematical optimisation.

## My Role

I participated in the setup, data collection, analysis, and report production.

## Process

Five inclination angles were tested. For each angle, ten repetitions were performed and averaged to improve accuracy. Landing coordinates were recorded and outliers removed.

## Technical / Design Decisions

The coefficient of restitution was estimated using vertical free-fall rebound tests. The derived equation was differentiated to estimate the optimal angle.

## Result

The report explained the experimental result and reflected on additional influencing factors.

## Reflection

This project developed my ability to connect physical testing, mathematical modelling, and uncertainty in experimental results.
