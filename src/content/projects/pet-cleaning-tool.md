## Overview

Shared animal-care research led to four individual concepts. My PetEase proposal addressed repeated tool switching through a modular handheld cleaning system. The team selected Preme's RePooPress for development; power and heating constraints led that concept to become Dooti.

## Outcome

PetEase reached concept sketches, evaluation and detailed CAD. Dooti reached an integrated pull–seal–cut prototype, a reusable pouch, packaging and exhibition testing.

## My Role

I designed PetEase individually. As Team 14's CIO, I contributed to Dooti's appearance and functional CAD, circuit design, power calculations, electronics testing, component arrangement and assembly integration. The final Dooti product and presentation were collaborative work.

## Process

The team conducted eight interviews across four countries, covering three shelters, two fosterers and three pet owners, alongside a survey with over 30 participants. I explored 15 PetEase ideas and shortlisted three concepts before choosing the dual-head direction.

## Technical / Design Decisions

PetEase combines a twist-lock attachment, removable fur cartridge, passive activated-carbon compartment and simple controls. Dooti replaces a large-area heat press with a roll-fed system that seals the inner cellulose film along a narrow line.

## Result

PetEase remains a concept. Dooti demonstrates prototype integration, with broader seal, handling, pouch and material validation still required. The packaging's proposed shelter collaboration is an academic brand concept.

## Reflection

The project connected individual product architecture to team engineering. Earlier power calculations, component-fit trials and concept-selection deadlines would have protected more time for prototyping and testing.
