## Overview

This individual project investigated customer needs in the sneaker industry and developed a modular shoe concept from research through final presentation.

## Outcome

The project produced 15 hand-drawn concept sketches, a physical conceptual prototype, final renderings, a detailed PDF, and a poster exhibited at a university showcase.

## My Role

I completed the project individually, covering research, sketching, concept development, prototype construction, rendering, and presentation.

## Process

I analysed popular sneaker products to identify strengths and areas for improvement. I then developed multiple concepts and built a prototype using deconstructed old shoes, cardboard, and fabric.

## Technical / Design Decisions

The prototype focused on communicating modularity and functionality with accessible materials rather than attempting a production-ready shoe.

## Result

The final concept was presented through renderings, analysis, and exhibition materials.

## Reflection

This project helped me practice translating market observations into a physical product concept with a clear visual story.
